BookUp IQ is the operating layer for private clinics. It captures every enquiry, books it on the right doctor's calendar, reminds the patient and shows the owner where revenue is lost.

Office Management MVP · Hatchup Capital · November 2026

The problem

Clinics lose revenue between their ads,
WhatsApp and the front desk.

1

Slow replies

A patient asks a price on WhatsApp. The answer comes hours later, after they booked somewhere else.

2

Forgotten follow-ups

Nobody owns the lead. "I'll think about it" is never called back.

3

No-shows

Booked consultations are not confirmed or reminded. The doctor's slot stays empty.

4

No ad-to-revenue link

The owner pays for ads but cannot see which campaign brought paid treatments.

Today: WhatsApp Business on one phone, a paper or Google calendar, and spreadsheets. Who we serve: aesthetic, dental, hair, cosmetic and longevity clinics with 5–10 doctors in Dubai, Istanbul and Riyadh.

Who uses it

Four staff roles. Each sees only their job.

Roles are checked in the screen and again on the server, so a hidden button is also a refused action.

Manager

Clinic owner or practice manager
  • Sets up clinic, doctors, services
  • Watches revenue and the funnel
  • Runs marketing, approves refunds
Starts on: Today (manager view)

Coordinator

Patient coordinator or sales
  • Works leads and the pipeline
  • Replies on WhatsApp
  • Books consultations, follows up
Starts on: Today, Inbox, Leads

Reception

Front desk
  • Checks patients in
  • Books and reschedules
  • Takes payments at the desk
Starts on: Calendar, Today

Doctor

Treating doctor
  • Sees their own day
  • Records the consult outcome
  • Books the treatment plan
Starts on: My day

The patient has no app and no login. They confirm, reschedule or pay a deposit from signed links sent on WhatsApp.

MVP scope

What the MVP must prove

  • Capture 100% of inbound leads into one patient record, with no duplicates by phone.
  • First reply within 5 minutes in working hours, with an owner assigned automatically.
  • Book, reschedule or cancel in under 60 seconds, with no double booking across doctors and Google Calendar.
  • Fewer no-shows with WhatsApp confirmations and reminders.
  • One funnel from lead source to booked, attended and paid, per doctor, service and campaign.

How we measure it

Median first-response time< 5 min
Leads with no reply after 24 h< 5%
Leads with an owner and a next action100%
Lead → consultation booking+20%
No-show rate−30%
Bookings that match the doctor's real calendar100%

Proposed targets, agreed with each pilot clinic and measured against its own baseline.
